> A curated list of resources for healthcare analytics, clinical data mining, population health intelligence, health outcome modeling, and hospital operations analytics.

Healthcare analytics leverages statistical modeling, machine learning, and data engineering to transform clinical, operational, and financial health data into actionable insights for improved patient care. This list is intended for health data analysts, biostatisticians, clinical data scientists, and healthcare operations leads.

## Clinical Data Mining & NLP

Natural language processing pipelines, text mining tools, and feature extraction frameworks tailored for clinical notes and unstructured electronic health records (EHR).

- [cTaKES (Apache clinical Text Analysis and Knowledge Extraction System)](https://github.com/apache/ctakes) — Open-source NLP system for extraction of information from electronic medical record clinical free-text.
- [spark-nlp-healthcare](https://github.com/JohnSnowLabs/spark-nlp-healthcare) — Specialized NLP suite for medical entity recognition, clinical assertion status detection, and entity resolution.
- [scispaCy](https://github.com/allenai/scispacy) — Python package containing spaCy models for processing biomedical, clinical, and scientific text.
- [MIMIC-Code](https://github.com/MIT-LCP/mimic-code) — Open repository of code and SQL scripts for processing and analyzing the MIMIC critical care database.

## Population Health & Epidemiology

Analytical tools, registries, and spatial packages for tracking community health outcomes, disease prevalence, and social determinants of health (SDoH).

- [EpiStats](https://github.com/epistats) — Suite of R packages designed for epidemiological data collection, outbreak investigation, and sample survey analysis.
- [SurveillanceR](https://github.com/surveillance-r) — Open framework for statistical analysis of public health surveillance data and infectious disease outbreak detection.
- [SDoH-Data-Harmonizer](https://github.com/ahrq) — Data pipelines for integrating social determinants of health metrics into clinical analytics workflows.

## Hospital Operations & Capacity Analytics

Operations research engines, queueing models, and scheduling optimization algorithms designed for hospital efficiency and resource utilization.

- [Bed-Management-Simulator](https://github.com/hospital-ops) — Simulation framework for forecasting emergency department throughput, bed availability, and patient flow.
- [Nurse-Scheduling-Optimizer](https://github.com/coin-or) — Mathematical optimization models for solving complex clinical shift scheduling and staffing constraints.
- [OR-Capacity-Planner](https://github.com/open-operating-room) — Analytics engine designed to optimize operating room block scheduling and surgical suite utilization.

## Predictive Modeling & Risk Scoring

Machine learning algorithms and risk prediction frameworks validated for clinical decision support.

### Readmission & Mortality Prediction

- [EHR-ML-Pipeline](https://github.com/google/edouard) — End-to-end deep learning framework for building predictive clinical models on FHIR and EHR data sources.
- [LACE Index Calculator Engine](https://github.com/lace-index) — Algorithmic implementation of the LACE tool for predicting 30-day readmission and mortality risk.

### Disease Progression Modeling

- [Progression-Modeling-Kit](https://github.com/ohdsi-studies) — Analytics framework designed to model disease trajectories and treatment pathways over time across longitudinal patient cohorts.
